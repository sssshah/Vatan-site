Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vatan Indian Vegetarian Cuisine & Bakery ("Vatan," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit vatans.com, submit a reservation or inquiry, interact with our communications, or engage with our business profiles.

Information you provide

Depending on how you interact with us, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number, and preferred Vatan location
  • Reservation details, including the requested date, time, party size, and notes
  • Contact, catering, event, and order inquiry details
  • Your marketing preference when you choose whether to receive promotional email

Please do not include sensitive personal information in open-text fields.

Information collected automatically

When you use the website, we and our service providers may collect limited technical and usage information, including your IP address, browser and device type, referring page, pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, and interactions such as telephone or website-button clicks. Cookies or similar technologies may be used for website operation, measurement, security, and advertising attribution.

Google Business Profile reviews

Through the Google Business Profile API, we access reviews posted publicly to our own business profiles, including the reviewer name, star rating, review text, and review date. We use this information to monitor reviews, draft and post replies, and maintain an internal record of reviews and responses. Review information may be processed through Google, n8n workflow automation, and Anthropic's Claude API to support this process.

How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Respond to reservations, orders, catering requests, questions, and customer-service needs
  • Operate, secure, maintain, measure, and improve the website and our services
  • Send promotional email when you have chosen to receive it, with an option to unsubscribe
  • Respond to reviews and manage our business profiles
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and security incidents and comply with legal obligations

Service providers and third-party services

We use service providers to operate the website and related business processes. These may include Netlify for website hosting and form handling; Google for analytics, advertising measurement, business profiles, maps, and related services; MailerLite for optional email marketing; and n8n and Anthropic for review-response automation. These providers may process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy practices.

Ordering, delivery, maps, reviews, social media, and other links may take you to services operated by third parties, such as Clover. Information you submit directly to those services is governed by their privacy policies, not this policy.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that perform functions for us; with a Vatan location or authorized staff member handling your request; when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security; or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or sale. We do not authorize service providers to use information for purposes unrelated to the services they provide to us.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain business and customer-service records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary by the type of information and service involved. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of internet transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

  • You may unsubscribe from marketing email by using the unsubscribe link in the message.
  • You may adjust browser settings to limit cookies, although some website features may not work as intended.
  • You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information we maintain, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. The date at the top indicates when it was last revised.

Contact us

For questions or requests concerning this policy or your information, email info@vatans.com or use our Contact page.

This policy covers information handled through the Vatan website and the business processes described above. Separate third-party services maintain their own privacy policies.
