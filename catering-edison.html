Plan Your Menu

Weddings and family celebrations

Start with chaat or dhokla, then build a Gujarati meal around undhiyu, dal, rice, and rotli. Ask about live dosa or chaat stations when planning your reception or party.

Office lunches and meetings

A thali is a practical starting point for a work lunch. Share the guest count, when lunch needs to be served, and how much room your venue has for setup.

Jain and Swaminarayan options

Tell us which guests need Jain meals without root vegetables or Swaminarayan meals without onion or garlic so we can plan the right dishes and quantities.

Packages and pricing

Use the catering packages to compare menu options. Your quote will depend on the menu, guest count, and event details.

Catering FAQ

What should I include when requesting a quote?

Send your event date and time, venue address, estimated guest count, and dietary needs. If your venue has a delivery window or setup requirements, include those too.

Can I visit the Edison restaurant?

Yes. Find directions and restaurant details on the Edison location card. For event pricing and availability, call (201) 772-6994.
