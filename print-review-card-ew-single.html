How to use this file:
1. Open in Chrome or Safari
2. Wait a second for the QR code to generate (it appears automatically)
3. File → Print → set paper to Letter, margins to None or Minimum
4. The card sits in the top-left quadrant of the page — the two faint dashed lines mark the fold creases
5. Fold vertically along the middle line (bring the right half over onto the left half), then fold horizontally along the middle line (bring the bottom half up and over onto the top half). The card ends up as the top face, right-side up.
6. Print on cardstock for a stiffer standing tent card. Test the fold once — if it lands upside down or on the wrong face for your fold direction, let me know and I'll move the card to a different quadrant.
